## The rule that shapes tonight
"All project outputs and repositories must be created during the hackathon."
So tonight is installs, accounts and measured runtimes only. No pipeline code.

## How we are scored
A judge runs our skill on a case we have not seen.
  Judgment 40%  — clear reasoning behind the molecules we chose
  Execution 20% — a reproducible pipeline in a GitHub repo
  User fit 20%  — what the design is for, and who needs it
  Path to value 20% — a credible route to real use

## The thesis
Everyone generates hundreds of candidates. Almost nobody can defend the cut
from 400 down to 10. Our product is the funnel, not the generator — and the
agent stops when the next predicted gain falls below the spread between our
scoring sources, and says so.
